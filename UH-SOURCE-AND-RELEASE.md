# United Hampshire National Archives: maintained source

This repository is the maintained United Hampshire fork of [nationalarchives/ds-forms](https://github.com/nationalarchives/ds-forms). The original National Archives Git history, licence, application structure and technical provenance remain part of the repository.

- **Original source revision at adoption:** `524f929728453fbe5b7d251c853f80a10ac5e156`
- **Maintained development repository:** `bravogov/ds-forms`
- **Institution responsible for public content and service:** The National Archives (United Hampshire)
- **Application function:** Archives public enquiry, transfer and complaints form presentation
- **Public institutional website:** https://nationalarchives.gov.uhrblx.com/

## Development and delivery

Use the application's existing native testing tools. The inherited UK National Archives image publishing, release tagging and UK `ds-infrastructure-web` update workflow has been disconnected in this fork. GitHub source changes do not confer editorial or legal authority, and cannot promote themselves to any UK or UH production environment.

The current UH Archives public service continues on its established transitional release. This maintained fork is **not yet the source of its live production application**. Approval requires authenticated editor permissions, reviewed content/data transfer, backups and restore, migrations, native app integration, immutable GitHub-origin builds, acceptance and rollback, and a single approved UH delivery route. Do not create a parallel public editor or redirect production traffic to this fork before those requirements are met.

The Archives retains its own publication responsibility. Accord/Whitehall is the central-government editor, not the owner of Archives website pages. Formal licence terms and Crown copyright statements must be verified against competent UH authority before official publication; website text and repository changes do not create legal authority.
